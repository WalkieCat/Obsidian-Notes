Key lessons:
- Takes time to understand inherited database, codebase and domains to implement meaningful changes 
- The project scope must stay realistic and achievable, while prioritizing the most achievable deliverables and must also consider the sponsor's suggestion 
- Sponsor's feedback is important and can influence the development process > mention the share feature
- Documentation and version controls are essential for future maintainability and ownership

Future recommendations:
Group has some recommendations for future work:
- A integrated payment system for entire Fitness Frontline suite for easier management and distribution
- Future team could focus on improving overall website performance, example loading time for the survey results 
- The project is using outdated packages, which could lead to security concerns > future team is encouraged to update them 
- Further works could be new accessibility features, development for mobile devices

Conclusion:
- The group has improved My Fitness Record from an outdated platform to a modern and presentable health survey system
