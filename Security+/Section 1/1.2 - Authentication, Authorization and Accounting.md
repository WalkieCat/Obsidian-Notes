## AAA Framework:
- Identification:
	- Claim to be a user on the system
- Authentication:
	- The check between username, password or additional authentication factors to prove your are that person
- Authorization:
	- Determine the access that an user have based on identification and authentication
- Accounting:
	- Security logs about events happening 

## Authenticating systems
- Manage hundreds to thousands of systems that can never be seen
- The challenge becomes how to verify that the system that is logging on is a system that is authorized on the network
