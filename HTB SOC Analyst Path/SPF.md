## Sender Policy Framework:
An email authentication mechanism which allows only authorized senders to send on behalf of a domain, and prevents all unauthorized users from doing so.
SPF enables the receiving email server to check that an email claiming to come from a specific domain indeed comes from an IP address authorized by that domain's administrator.
