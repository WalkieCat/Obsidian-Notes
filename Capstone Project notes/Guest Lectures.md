# Craig from Canberra Innovation Network

- Innovation means adopting new things that will benefit people
- Some reasons that people don't want to innovate:
	- They are afraid of change
	- Cost concern (building the system, training new personnel, testing etc.)
	- Ecosystem (what if no one wants to use the product?)
	- How do you stand out from other people?

# Fundamental Method of Innovation:
## Learn from your customers:
- Innovation comes from listening to your customers and then using these information to improve your product
- **Find people to help** - think about the people that your product that wants to help 
- Look for the need of people - what do they want and what problem do they have
- Cool product =/= they will use that product
- **Ask people questions** - is your product solving their problem? if not then what can you do to improve it? ask open ended questions
- Talk to people and understand their needs, test your ideas and figure out some solutions

## For my project:
1. Who am I trying to help?
2. What is their problem?
3. What solution are you offering and why is it different from other people?
