**WEEK 2**
guest lecture 1: craig from canberra innovation network - talked about how to form ideas for your project based on innovation, questions to ask yourself and customers about your product, and how to form the basis of the ideas for the product

guest lecture 2: 
previous students with an award winning project to share their experience - capstone replicates the industry experience where scope changes in an instant and all responsibilities fall on you. 
the best team have the best communication, and planning and communication matter as much as technical work.
define the Minimum Viable Product and scope clearly. communicate with the sponsors to validate the scope to reduce the ambiguity on what the final deliverable is/are
get something working as early as possible - get the proof of concept out of the door and iterate again 
find a realistic scope and align your deliverable with your sponsor
set a milestone 2 days before every deadline to protect against failure 
spend 80% building the project and 20% refining the product (last 2 week for example)
assign deliverable a lead who is responsible for the entire deliverable and not just their part 
clear communication and be honest with other groups, and be understanding of other people's struggle
documentation is another crucial part of the project and should be started immediately if possible
the documentation shows why we choose the solution and what are the pros/cons of the solution
document early - informal is fine but it needed to be documented - if it is not written then it is not done
the documentation should be made such that someone who is not technical can understand the product in detail
understand your sponsor company (who they are, what product they are offering, what are their customer market) and ask the sponsor their vision of the product - what they want the product to do
