## Management and leadership:
- A manager is a member of the org who is assigned to several roles such as marshal and administer resources to complete the desire objective
- Management: achieving mission objective using a given set of resources
- The roles could be interpersonal, informational or decisional

## Leadership and Management
- A leader influences employee so that they are willing to achieve the mission, is expected to lead by example and demonstrate traits that instill a desire to follow
- A manager administer resources required for the organization
- Sometimes their tasks intertwin with each other although they often aren't responsible for personnel

## Management characteristics
- Basic approaches:
	1. Traditional theory uses the core principles of planning, organizing, staffing, directing and controlling (POSDC)
	2. Popular theory categorizes the principle of management into planning, organizing, leading and controlling (POLC)

## Management of Information Security
- IS program begins with policies, standards and practices which form the foundation for the information security architecture
- The InfoSec team is similar to a management but is more focused on the secure operation of the org
- Some time this mission might conflict or require resolution against IT management goals
- InfoSec prioritize confidentiality, integrity and availability of information

## Solving problems:
Identify the problem > gather information and make assumptions > develop solutions > analyze and compare solutions > select, implement and evaluation solution

## Principle of IS management:
Six Ps:
- Planning
- Policy
- Programs
- Protection
- People
- Project Management

## Information security planning and governance
- Long term strategic planning focuses the org resources on clearly defined goals
- Leadership of IS is best achieved through governance, risk management and compliance, ensuring InfoSec objectives are addressed at the highest level 
- The board of directors provides governance review and oversight for IS, which then applies governance principles to security, emphasizing the responsibility of the board and senior management for effective oversight

## Information security strategy
- Some principles: CIA elements, compliance with rules and regulations, maintaining a security baseline appropriate for the org
- Security strategy should reflect the org mission, strategy and business model
- Decision makers should be knowledgeable about the org strategy and management system 
- Managers should provide the required technical inputs for the decision
