## Enterprise architecture and information system architecture

**Enterprise architecture**:
- Enterprise architecture is a management and technology practice that is used to increase the performance of a enterprise by allowing them to view their company and their strategic directions, business practices, information flow ands and technology resources. Using a current and future versions of this integrated view, an org can manage their transition to different operating states. [^1] 

**Information system architecture**:
- The act of designing an information system in by "information architects" in such a way that information is organized to make it findable, manageable and useful to all levels from infrastructure to user interface. [^2]

## Importance of Architecture in ERP implementation
- Using enterprise architecture can offer a framework to address present requirements, connect with existing system and adapt to future system. This is a comprehensive framework that encompass the organizational structure, business processes, information technology, and human factors. The framework recognize their inter-connection in an ecosystem. [^3]
