## Preview:
- Systems integration means to have a system with multiple types of hardware to work seamlessly together (key issue)
- [[ERP Systems]], a major information system allows organization to integrate multiple systems into one company-wide application with a database management system

## Functional Silos:
### Definition:
- Isolated and compartmentalized operating systems
- Often divided into 2 types: 
	- [[Horizontal]]
	- [[Vertical]] 

## Business Process and Silos:
- Functional silos gave birth to business process re-engineering (PBR)
- Cross-functional business process can involve people and resources working together and sharing information at any level of the org
- This structure breaks the functional silos workflow by opening up the information flow for other departments

## Evolution of Information Systems in Orgs
- The evolution of IS suggest that it has generally been used to support the demands of information that is vital to the functions of the org's smooth structure, which the silo information system is inefficient at

## IS Architecture
- The need for new IS models is the direct result of advancement in computer and networking technologies
- Today web-based system used a distributed architecture which allow users to connect to a server that provides a window into the application and the database, and allows the shared use of the application and information

## IS Functionalization
- IS supports other business functions such as manufacturing, marketing, etc. which all have different information needs and requirements
- Each functional area also has multiple levels of management which also requires different level of analysis and information

## Systems Integration 
### Logical:
- IS that allows orgs to share data with all stakeholders based on needs and authorization
- Change to org structures, processes, roles and responsibilities changes are needed
### Physical:
- Seamless connectivity between heterogenous systems
- BPR changes the mindset of employees to encourage and enable them to do their tasks in a new way

### Steps:
| Steps | Name                              | Example/Why                                                                                   |
| ----- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Resource Categorization           | Instituting IT support for integrated systems to avoid support and maintenance problems       |
| 2     | Compliance and Standard           | Develop SSO for all parties to sign in anywhere, any time                                     |
| 3     | Legacy System                     | Develop policies to support legacy systems                                                    |
| 4     | Middleware Tools                  | Temporary tools to host existing applications for the org in the process of integration       |
| 5     | Authentication and Authorization  | SSO for application and data for all time authorized access                                   |
| 6     | Centralized IT support            | IT support staff needs to be able to support all applications and platforms                   |
| 7     | Backup, Recovery and Security     | Good backup and recovery system in case of major system failure or disaster                   |
| 8     | Hard and software standardization | Develop organizational standards for all software and hardware aligning with the org strategy |

### Benefits and limitations of systems integration

| Benefits                         | Limitations                                                |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Increased revenue and growth     | High initial cost                                          |
| Leveling competitive environemtn | Power and inter-department conflict to information sharing |
| Enhanced information visibility  | Long term and intangible ROI                               |
| Increased standardization        | Restricts creativity and indepedence                       |
