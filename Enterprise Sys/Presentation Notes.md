Slide 2:
- RIC - Regional Investment Corporation is an Australian Government body created to deliver concessional loans to farmers and farm-related business nationwide to support climate resilience and promote growth of the agriculture sector
- Created under Regional Investment Corporation Act 2018 to manage 4 billion dollars across 2 loan schemes, the Farm Business Concessional Loans and National Water Infrastructure Loan 
- Till ate, the RIC has delivered over 2900 loans valued at 3.17 billion dollars, with 68 staff distributed nationally
- RIC has significantly expanded its original mission, with two new loan schemes for drought relief and marine incidents, along with additional fundings and new responsibilities for climate resilience and net-zero objectives
- It also carries an obligation to report to 2 Ministers, Finance and Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries as a Commonwealth body

Slide 3:
Why must RIC needed to change? There are 3 problems that necessitated this change:
- Growing responsibilities from expanding mission scopes with new loan schemes, fundings and objectives
- The manual setup that RIC was using was not able to support this new demand and as such, the limitation of this system was beginning to appear
- Compliance pressure due to legal obligations to report to the 2 Ministers
- Must demonstrate compliance to government standards > manual reporting increases the risks of errors > increases possibilities of compliance failure with serious consequences
- Most critical - risking RIC's core mission > RIC exist to deliver loans to farmers in need within a reasonable timeframe > but with fragmented internal systems and staff being overwhelmed with manual tasks > response time suffers significantly
These problems are what made an integrated ERP solution necessary
