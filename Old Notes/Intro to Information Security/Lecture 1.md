## **Intro:**
- Every org has information that it wants to protect and it is their responsibility to protect this information 
- security refers to the actions taken to make something safe from harm 
- in infosec, this refers to security management, data security and network security 
- we uses the [[CIA Triad]] to ensure the safety of information  
- human is the missing link to solve the challenges to improve Information Security 

## **Risks**:
- fundamentally, infosec concerns risks management. they have 2 dimension:
	1. Impact
	2. Likelihood

- the main idea is to take informed risk and manage those risks, rather than taking all risks

- some of these statistics:

	- company hides their security failures
- with the Privacy Legislation Amendment Bill 2022, the penalties cap for data breach has been increased from $2.22 million AUD to whichever is greater of:
	- $50 million AUD
	- three times the benefit attained from the misuse of the information
	- 30% of a company adjusted turnover in the period of the breach
