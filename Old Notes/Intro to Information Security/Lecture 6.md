Security control:
Measure to manage risk to an organization assets
Control helps protect the CIA of Information
Controls can operate at different stage

Classification by function:
Preventive: stop a threat before it occurs
Detective: identify a security incident
Corrective: mitigate/repair after incident
Effective cybersecurity requires an implementation of all three 

Classification by scope:
Technical: through technology and system 
Operation: through processes, people and everyday operations
Management: through governance and risks
Each scope complement the other 

Policy vs. Practice:
Frameworks provide high-level control but rarely the technical details 
The challenge is translating the intent into technical implementation through documented procedures, standards and tools

NIST SP 800-37 - Risk management framework
Integrates information security and risk management activities into SDL with 6 steps:
Categorise - select - implement - assess - authorize - monitoring (explanation on slides)

NIST SP 800-53 - Comprehensive control catalogue
Provides detailed catalogue of security and privacy controls applicable to all federal information systems and critical infrastructures. Includes 20 control families including:
Access control - audit and accountability - system and communications protection - risk assessments - configuration management
Each family include specific control statements, objective and guidance

Rmf and control implementation
The rmf step relies on NIST SP 800-53 catalogue and implement operationalize the controls in an organization
Structure mirrors ISO/IEC 27001 Plan-do-check-act model 
Each controls acts as a system of governance and audit that must be continuously reviewed and validated

Information security manual
Definitive Aussie government standard for cybersecurity published by ASD 
Defines principles to help organizations "govern protect detect respond and recover", more information on slides

Essential Eight (E8)
Prioritized eight mitigation strategies by the ACSC
Mandatory subset of controls drawn directly from the broader ISM 
Each E8 controls corresponds directly to one or more ISM technical controls
This defines what to implement first and how to measure effectiveness; ISM defines how to technically configure it 
They form the practical layer of Protective Security Policy Framework
This is a continuous improvement journey and the maturity model provide a clear roadmap for organizations to progressively enhance their defense against complicated threat
